# Examples

Real videos of Built to Click, edited with what is in this repository. Re-encoded at 720x1280 (CRF 23, capped at
1.5 Mbps) to keep the repository light; the originals are 1080x1920 and 4K. **Play them with sound on the
[gallery page](https://builttoclick.github.io/video-editing-playbook/).**

All rights reserved for everything in this folder (see [LICENSE](../LICENSE)): watch and link, do not reuse.

## Videos

| File | What it is | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| [`videos/vsl-v2.mp4`](videos/vsl-v2.mp4) | The VSL for our landing page, 2:23, Spanish. One raw take of 17:50, pre-edited by code into CapCut, cut by Joaquín, every graphic coded in Remotion, masters exported in code ([docs/11](../docs/11-motion-graphics-in-code.md), [docs/14](../docs/14-masters-and-web.md)) | the name behind the speaker's head at 0:01; the two phones (the average page on the right); the board swinging in at 0:34; the search typing itself on the words; captions one word at a time |
| [`videos/raw-vs-final.mp4`](videos/raw-vs-final.mp4) | The first 9.9 s of the VSL: left the raw phone file as recorded, right the finished video, frame for frame, with the finished audio | the same frame on both sides (the cut is exact), the clean mic sound, what the overlays add |
| [`videos/ad-V6-01.mp4`](videos/ad-V6-01.mp4) | A Meta ad, 35.3 s: hook "Las dos de la mañana" + body "how people buy today" + closing "te muestro" | the hook card in the first second, overlays by piece from Claude Design, captions in the house style |
| [`videos/ad-V6-03.mp4`](videos/ad-V6-03.mp4) | A Meta ad, 35.9 s: a client got a lead from ChatGPT | the ChatGPT box typing, the flags (Brazil, Europe, Paraguay) landing on the words, a client's page with the words that matter boxed, the "42 personas desde ChatGPT" chip |
| [`videos/ad-V6-04.mp4`](videos/ad-V6-04.mp4) | A Meta ad, 38.5 s: a case study, people who found a business on Google without knowing it | the Google bar typing ("a search is shown as a search"), the client's page before and after with no name on it, the visits counting up, the contact button circled |

The three ads were pre-edited by code and edited by Joaquín in CapCut ([docs/07](../docs/07-capcut-pre-edit.md)),
with overlays designed in Claude Design and rendered locally with real alpha ([docs/10](../docs/10-overlays-claude-design.md)).
They are the ones of that series that do not say a price.
